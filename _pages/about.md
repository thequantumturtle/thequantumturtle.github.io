# Quantum computing is easier to understand when you can actually do something with it.

I’m **Daniel Justice**, a quantum computing educator, researcher, and builder at Carnegie Mellon University. I work on how we **learn, evaluate, and prepare for quantum technology**—from programming quantum computers to post-quantum readiness and practical technical judgment.

**The Quantum Turtle** is where I share the work: courses, experiments, explanations, classroom resources, research, and the occasional attempt to make an emerging technology feel a little less mysterious.

## What I’m working on

My work sits at the intersection of **quantum computing, software engineering, machine learning, and technology readiness**.

A recurring question in my research and public work is not simply *“Can this technology work?”* but *“Compared to what, useful for what, and what has to be true for people and organizations to use it well?”*

That includes work on quantum machine learning evaluation, quantum software, post-quantum cryptography readiness, and how organizations make sound decisions around emerging technology.

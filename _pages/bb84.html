What do the arrows mean?

The arrows are a simple state map, not the path a photon travels. On a Bloch sphere, |0⟩ and |1⟩ point in opposite directions along Z. |+⟩ and |−⟩ point in opposite directions along X.

Sifted bits (not a secure key)

The table reveals all bits for learning. Real BB84 tests a random sample of matching-basis bits, discards those tested bits, and applies error correction and privacy amplification. The bits shown here are not a secure cryptographic key.

Why does this work?

In the + basis, bit 0 is |0⟩ and bit 1 is |1⟩. In the × basis, bit 0 is |+⟩ and bit 1 is |−⟩.

In the ideal model, measuring in the preparation basis always gives the encoded bit. The other basis gives either bit with equal chance. An eavesdropper can disturb states by measuring and resending them.

This browser exercise uses classical messages to coordinate an idealized simulation. It cannot transmit real qubits. Because Alice computes the simulated outcomes after Bob shares his bases, it requires honest participants and cannot enforce quantum security.

With an ideal intercept-resend attack, Eve causes an average error rate of 25% among the bits whose bases match. Small experiments vary because measurements are random.

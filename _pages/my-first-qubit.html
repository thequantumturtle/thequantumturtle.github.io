How the two sliders work

Probability Mix (θ) sets the chances of measuring 0 or 1. Watch the circles grow and shrink. Relative Phase (φ) turns the clock hand in the |1⟩ circle. The |0⟩ hand stays still so you can compare them. When either circle is empty, changing the phase has no effect on the qubit.

Each circle’s filled area shows the chance of that result. The sliders can show any pure state of one qubit. A mixed state needs a different model.

Try it: Start at |0⟩. Press H → Z → H → MEASURE. You will get 1. Or set Probability Mix to 90°. Now 0 and 1 each have a 50% chance.

This browser toy is a first step toward a real wooden busy board. The real toy could use turning knobs and two small screens. This version runs on your computer, not on quantum hardware.

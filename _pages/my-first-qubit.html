|ψ⟩ = a|0⟩ + b|1⟩

|ψ⟩ = (1.00 + 0.00i)|0⟩ + (0.00 + 0.00i)|1⟩

Each coefficient shows its real and imaginary parts, even when zero. Values are rounded. θ = 0°.

Start at |0⟩. Try a slider or gate!

Try this first

  1. Start at |0⟩. The blue circle is full.
  2. Press H. Both circles show a 50% chance.
  3. Press Z. The |1⟩ hand turns, but the chances stay the same.
  4. Press H again, then Measure. You will get 1.

What the board shows

Two circles show one qubit. Each circle's filled area shows the chance of measuring that result. Chance of 1 (θ) makes one circle grow as the other shrinks. Relative Phase (φ) turns the clock hand in the |1⟩ circle. The |0⟩ hand stays still so you can compare them. When either circle is empty, changing the phase does not change the state.

These sliders can show any pure state of one qubit. A mixed state needs a different model.

This browser toy is a first step toward a real wooden busy board. The real toy could use turning knobs and two small screens. It runs on your computer, not on quantum hardware.

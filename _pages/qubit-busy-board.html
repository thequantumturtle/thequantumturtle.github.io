Try this first

  1. Choose |0⟩. The arrow points up.
  2. Press H. The arrow moves to the middle. Now 0 and 1 each have a 50% chance.
  3. Press Z. The arrow turns, but the chances stay at 50/50.
  4. Press H again, then Measure. You will get 1.

What the board shows

This board shows one qubit as an arrow inside a sphere. The arrow tells you its state. X, Y, and Z turn the arrow around different axes. H turns it around a slanted axis. Each gate follows the correct path. Drag the sphere to see the arrow from a new angle. This changes your view, not the qubit. Probability Mix (θ) moves the arrow between the top and bottom. Relative Phase (φ) turns it around the middle. At the top or bottom, changing the phase does not change the state.

Try it: Start at |0⟩. Press H, then Measure. You may get 0 or 1. Each has a 50% chance. After you measure, the qubit stays at that result until a gate changes it.

This is a model of one qubit. It runs in your browser, not on a quantum computer.

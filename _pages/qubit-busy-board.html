The Qubit Busy Board

Push a button. Watch the qubit change. What happens when you measure?

Ready! Try pressing X or H.
0: 100%
1: 0%

The qubit begins at |0⟩, pointing up.

What are the buttons doing?

X flips the qubit, H rotates it into or out of a superposition, Z changes its phase, and Y rotates around another axis. Unlike an ordinary bit, the qubit can point in many directions. The sphere tracks a pure single-qubit state.

Try this: press H, then Measure. Reset and try again. You may get either answer. Measure collapses the state to |0⟩ or |1⟩. The bars show the probabilities before you measure, and update after collapse.

A touch-friendly educational simulation of ideal gates and computational-basis measurement. No real quantum hardware is involved.
